Our Mission

Strength in Speaking exists to help break the silence surrounding men's mental health.


Too many men feel they have to hide their struggles, pretend everything is fine or face difficult times alone.


We believe speaking up is a sign of strength. Everyone deserves to be heard, supported and reminded that their life matters.
