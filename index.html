Our Mission

Strength in Speaking exists to help break the silence surrounding men's mental health.


Too many men feel they have to hide their struggles, pretend everything is fine or face difficult times alone.


We believe speaking up is a sign of strength. Everyone deserves to be heard, supported and reminded that their life matters.

You can contact Strength in Speaking without entering your name or email address. Your message is private and is not posted publicly.

Please read before sending

This is a peer-support contact route, not a counselling or emergency service. Messages may not be read immediately and no immediate response is guaranteed. Do not use this form if you or someone else is in immediate danger: call 999 or go to A&E.

You do not need to identify yourself, but absolute anonymity cannot be promised. Cloudflare and the website may process technical information such as your IP address and the time of your visit to operate and protect the service. The site stores message content so a reply can be provided. Do not include details that identify you if you do not want to share them.
